Slab Leak Detection in North Mountain Village, Phoenix

A slab leak is a leak in a water line that runs under or inside a concrete slab foundation. It is hard to see, so detection equipment is used to pinpoint it.

Call Now for Free Local Help

Signs of a slab leak

Look for unexplained high water bills, the sound of running water when fixtures are off, warm spots on the floor, damp flooring or cracks. Many Phoenix-area homes are built on slabs, so this problem is common to check.

How a slab leak is located

Providers may use electronic listening devices, pressure testing or thermal imaging to find the exact point before opening the slab. This is a more specific form of water leak detection.

Repair options

Depending on the leak, a provider may suggest spot repair, rerouting the line or repiping. Compare options with the provider and see our overview of pipe leak repair.

Frequently asked questions

Is a slab leak an emergency?

It can be, because it can cause water damage and waste water. Shut off the main valve if needed and call promptly.

Can slab leaks be found without breaking concrete?

Detection tools can often narrow the location before any concrete is opened.
