Water Leak Detection in North Mountain Village, Phoenix

Water leak detection is the process of locating a hidden leak in supply lines, drains or fixtures without tearing out walls or floors first.

Signs you need water leak detection

A higher water bill, a meter that moves with everything off, damp drywall, peeling paint, warped flooring or a musty smell are all reasons to call. Small leaks often stay hidden until damage appears.

How providers find the leak

Providers commonly use pressure tests, acoustic listening equipment, moisture meters and thermal imaging to narrow down the source. If the leak is under concrete, see our guide to slab leak detection. Once found, pipe leak repair options can be discussed.

What does it cost?

Cost varies by the size of the home, access to the pipes and the method used. Ask the provider for a diagnostic fee and estimate before work begins.

When to call

Call as soon as you suspect a leak, and shut off the main water valve if water is actively flowing. For leak help from a local team, start at our leak detection and repair in North Mountain Village page or call (602) 836-3562.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if I have a hidden water leak?

Check whether your water meter keeps moving when all fixtures are off. Also look for damp spots, stains, musty odors and unexplained bill increases.

Should I turn off my water?

If water is actively flowing or flooding, shut off the main valve and call a provider.
