About Leak Detection Pro

Leak Detection Pro provides water, slab, and gas leak detection and pipe repair solutions across North Mountain Village and Phoenix, AZ, utilizing advanced acoustic, thermal, and electronic diagnostic technologies.

What we do

When you face unexplained water bills, foundation dampness, or suspected gas leaks, finding a qualified specialist quickly is essential. We answer calls 24/7, assess the nature of your leak problem, and immediately connect you with an independent local provider who possesses the specific diagnostic tools required for your situation. Learn more on our North Mountain Village leak detection overview.

Modern diagnostic standard

We advocate for non-invasive, damage-free leak location. Connected providers carry advanced technology rigs, including:

  • Acoustic ground microphones: Listening through tiles and concrete for pressurized water escaping supply lines.
  • FLIR thermal imaging cameras: Detecting temperature gradients and moisture migration behind walls and underneath slabs.
  • Digital combustible gas sniffers: PPM electronic gas detectors verifying line integrity.
  • Electromagnetic line locators: Mapping subterranean pipe pathways before any excavation.

Transparency & licensing disclosure

Leak Detection Pro is not a plumbing or gas engineering contractor. Work is performed exclusively by independent third-party contractors who establish their own pricing, diagnostic fees, and guarantees. In the State of Arizona, contractors must be licensed through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC). We encourage all callers to verify contractor licensing and bonding at roc.az.gov prior to approving work.
