About North Mountain Village Leak Detection

North Mountain Village Leak Detection provides dedicated water, slab, and gas leak detection and pipe repair solutions across North Mountain Village and Phoenix, AZ, using advanced acoustic, thermal, and electronic diagnostic technologies.

What we do

When you face unexplained water bills, foundation dampness, or suspected gas leaks, fast and accurate testing is essential. Our North Mountain Village team answers calls 24/7, evaluates the symptoms of your leak problem, and dispatches our local diagnostic specialists equipped for your specific situation. Learn more on our North Mountain Village leak detection overview.

Modern diagnostic standard

We specialize in non-invasive, damage-free leak location. Our service vehicles carry advanced diagnostic rigs, including:

  • Acoustic ground microphones: Listening through tiles and concrete for pressurized water escaping supply lines.
  • FLIR thermal imaging cameras: Detecting temperature gradients and moisture migration behind drywall and underneath slabs.
  • Digital combustible gas sniffers: PPM electronic gas detectors verifying line integrity.
  • Electromagnetic line locators: Mapping subterranean pipe pathways before any excavation.

Arizona ROC licensing & transparency standard

In the State of Arizona, residential plumbing and gas projects exceeding $1,000 in labor and materials require active licensing through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC). Our technicians provide clear written diagnostic and repair estimates prior to starting work, and homeowners can always verify active contractor licensing and bonding at roc.az.gov.
