- [Slab Leak Detection](https://www.northmountainvillageleakdetectionaz.com/slab-leak-detection/): Precision subterranean acoustic listening and thermal tracking for pressurized water pipes beneath concrete foundation slabs.

## Advanced Detection Technologies Used
- **Acoustic Ground Microphones & Frequency Analyzers**: High-amplification listening sensors that isolate subterranean water leak hiss and vibration frequencies through concrete, tile, and soil.
- **FLIR High-Resolution Infrared Thermal Imaging**: Non-invasive thermographic cameras identifying subtle thermal anomalies and moisture evaporation behind drywall, ceilings, and under slab foundations.
- **Digital Combustible Gas Sniffers & CGI Meters**: Highly sensitive electronic sniffers measuring parts-per-million (PPM) concentrations to pinpoint micro-leaks in natural gas and propane lines.
- **Electromagnetic Line Locators & Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR)**: Precise mapping of subterranean metallic and non-metallic pipes and conduits before any concrete cutting or excavation.
- **Digital Pressure Decay & Isolation Manometers**: Testing isolated plumbing loops to quantify pressure drops without destructive digging.
- **HD Video Sewer & Borescope Inspection**: Flexible fiber-optic cameras with sonde radio transmitters navigating interior drain, sewer, and supply lines.

## Emergency Protocols
- **Gas Leak Emergency**: Leave premises immediately. Avoid electrical switches, open flames, or cell phone use indoors. From outdoors, call 911 and Southwest Gas emergency lines before scheduling repairs.
- **Active Water Flooding**: Shut off the main water shutoff valve (usually at the street meter box or exterior hose bib connection) immediately, then call (602) 836-3562 for emergency dispatch.
