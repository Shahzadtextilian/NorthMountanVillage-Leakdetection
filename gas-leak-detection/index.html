Gas Leak Detection in North Mountain Village, Phoenix

If you smell gas, leave the building immediately and call 911 and your gas utility from outside. Do not call a referral line first.

Safety first

Do not use light switches, phones inside, lighters or appliances. Do not try to find the leak yourself. Wait outside until emergency responders say it is safe.

Signs of a gas leak

Common signs are a rotten-egg odor, a hissing sound near a gas line or appliance, bubbling in standing water outdoors, and dead plants near a buried line.

After the emergency

Once the area is safe and the utility has responded, an independent provider can test gas lines and appliance connections and repair or replace damaged piping. We can connect you with a provider through our main local leak detection service line at (602) 836-3562.

Water and gas lines are different problems

Gas leaks need specialized testing and strict safety steps. If your problem is moisture rather than odor, read about water leak detection instead.
