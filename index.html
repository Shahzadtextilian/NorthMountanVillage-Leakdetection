Signs you may have a hidden leak

The most common signs are a water bill that rises with no change in use, a water meter that keeps moving when every fixture is off, damp or warm spots on floors, musty smells, stained walls, and the sound of running water in a quiet house. For gas, warning signs include a rotten-egg odor, hissing near a line, or dead vegetation near a buried line. A suspected gas leak is an emergency, so see our gas leak guidance first.

Yes. Leave the building right away, avoid switches and flames, and call 911 and your gas utility from outside. Do not wait for a referral call.
