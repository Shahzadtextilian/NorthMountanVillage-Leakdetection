What happens after you call?

  1. You speak directly with our North Phoenix team and describe symptoms (water bill spike, meter movement, warm floor, odor).
  2. We dispatch our leak detection technician equipped with non-invasive acoustic, thermal, or gas detection instruments.
  3. Our specialist pinpoints the leak and provides a clear written diagnostic and repair estimate before performing any repair work.
