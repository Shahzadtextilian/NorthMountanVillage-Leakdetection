Pipe leak repair fixes or replaces a damaged section of water or gas piping after the leak has been located.

Repair after detection

Repairs usually follow a diagnosis. If you have not found the source yet, start with water leak detection or gas leak detection.

Common repair approaches

Providers may patch or replace a section of pipe, replace a fitting or valve, or recommend repiping when damage is widespread. The right choice depends on pipe material, age and access.

Questions to ask a provider

Ask for the license number, a written estimate, whether permits are needed and whether work is guaranteed. Verify licenses with the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for pipe repair?

It depends on the scope of work. Ask the provider whether a permit applies.

Is repairing or repiping better?

Spot repairs fit isolated damage. Repiping is considered when problems keep recurring. Ask for both options.
