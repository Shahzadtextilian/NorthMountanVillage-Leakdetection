Service Network & Contractor Standards

Leak Detection Pro coordinates a specialized local service network connecting residential and commercial property owners in North Mountain Village and the Greater Phoenix metropolitan area with trusted, independent local plumbing and leak detection specialists. All physical diagnostic inspections, electronic acoustic testing, quotes, and repairs are executed directly by independent licensed contractors within our local network.

When you call (602) 836-3562, your service request is routed to an independent local specialist dispatched to your neighborhood. Technicians provide clear, upfront diagnostics and work estimates before any physical plumbing labor commences.

Arizona Contractor Licensing (ROC Standards)

Under Arizona Revised Statutes, any residential plumbing or repair work where the aggregate contract price (including labor and materials) exceeds $1,000 must be performed by a contractor licensed by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC).

We require independent service providers in our service network to maintain applicable state and municipal licenses, bonding, and liability insurance. Customers are always encouraged to independently verify the active license standing of any contractor prior to signing work authorizations by visiting roc.az.gov.

Emergency Gas Leak Notice

If you smell a strong rotten-egg sulfur odor, hear hissing near a gas appliance or meter, or suspect an active natural gas or propane leak, evacuate the building immediately. Do not operate light switches, phones, or open flames. Once safely outdoors, call 911 and Southwest Gas at (877) 860-6020 before requesting post-emergency diagnostic services.

Pricing, Estimates & Warranties

Diagnostic fees, repair pricing, warranties, and scopes of work are established independently by each contracted provider. Always request a clear, written estimate detailing the diagnostic methodology and expected repair costs prior to commencing work.
