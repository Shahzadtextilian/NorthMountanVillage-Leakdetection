Arizona Contractor Licensing (ROC Verification Notice)

Under Arizona Revised Statutes, any residential plumbing or repair project where the aggregate contract price (including labor and materials) exceeds $1,000 must be performed by a contractor actively licensed by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC).

Our diagnostic and repair operations adhere to applicable Arizona state and City of Phoenix municipal licensing, bonding, and liability insurance standards. Homeowners are always encouraged to verify active contractor licensing and bonding status directly on the official ROC portal at roc.az.gov.

Emergency Gas Leak Notice

If you smell a strong rotten-egg sulfur odor, hear hissing near a gas appliance or meter, or suspect an active natural gas or propane leak, evacuate the building immediately. Do not operate light switches, phones, or open flames. Once safely outdoors, call 911 and Southwest Gas at (877) 860-6020 before requesting post-emergency diagnostic services.
