Services Available Across All Covered Areas

Independent providers in our network bring specialized, non-invasive electronic diagnostic equipment directly to your property:

  • Water Leak Detection: Pinpoint hidden water leaks behind walls, under floors, and within ceiling cavities using acoustic disc sensors and FLIR thermal imaging.
  • Slab Leak Detection: Non-destructive electronic listening to locate pressurized copper pipe pinholes under concrete foundations without exploratory digging.
  • Gas Leak Detection: Digital combustible gas sniffer testing (PPM) and pressure decay tests for interior and exterior natural gas and propane lines.
  • Pipe Leak Repair: Direct copper spot repairs, overhead PEX reroutes, and complete repiping services handled by licensed independent plumbing contractors.

Frequently asked questions about our coverage

How quickly can a specialist arrive at my location?

Because independent specialists are stationed throughout North Mountain Village, Sunnyslope, Moon Valley, Deer Valley, and Glendale, technicians can often arrive quickly for urgent leak situations.

Are technicians licensed to perform plumbing in Arizona?

Yes. All independent contractors perform diagnostic work and repairs in accordance with Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC) guidelines. Always request a written estimate and ROC verification before starting work.

What equipment do providers bring to my neighborhood?

Technicians arrive with dedicated diagnostic service vehicles carrying acoustic frequency listening meters, FLIR infrared thermal cameras, combustible gas sniffer meters, and digital pressure decay manometers.
